## 1. Frame contract

- Inclusion rule: include each Federal Reserve FOMC monetary-policy statement dated from 2018-01-01 through 2025-12-31 that announces a decision on the federal funds rate target or target range.
- Exclusion rule: exclude minutes, speeches, testimony, transcripts, projection materials, implementation notes, strategy-only statements, balance-sheet-only statements, liquidity-facility releases, and duplicate mirrors of the same decision identity.
- Date range: 2018-01-01 through 2025-12-31, inclusive, using the event date printed on the official Federal Reserve statement page.
- Official source family: Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System FOMC meeting calendars and information for 2021-2025; Board historical FOMC materials by year for 2018-2020; individual Board press-release statement pages for anchor proof.
- Why enumerable: FOMC policy statements are listed on official year calendars, and each listed statement page prints the release date, release time when available, and target-rate decision text. The frame is bounded by date, issuer, document type, and decision type.
- Claim ceiling: frame-complete within this bounded FOMC target-rate decision universe; not representative of all market events; no prevalence claim outside this frame.

This artifact intentionally stops before asset mapping, downstream eligibility, state acquisition, response measurement, or comparison work. It records candidate identity and provenance only.

2. For each meeting or intermeeting entry, follow only the official statement link that announces a federal funds rate target or target-range decision.
3. Retain one canonical event identity per decision date and target-range decision. Use the HTML press-release page as the canonical source reference, with its PDF version treated as a mirror.
4. Screen out official statement-like records that do not announce a target-rate or target-range decision, including longer-run strategy statements, balance-sheet-only implementation statements, liquidity-facility releases, and projection materials.
5. Reconcile year counts against the official calendar structure: eight regular decision statements in each year except 2020, where the March regular meeting is marked canceled after two emergency target-rate decisions and the year has nine target-rate decision statements.

Duplicate and non-frame record handling:

- Implementation notes released with the statements are operational companion documents, not separate target-rate decision identities.
- Strategy-only or balance-sheet-only statements on the official pages are screened out because they do not announce a federal funds rate target or target-range decision.

- Include only statement pages whose text announces a federal funds rate target or target-range decision.

- Collapse HTML/PDF mirrors and implementation notes to the same decision identity; do not promote mirrors as candidates.
- Preserve screened non-frame records in method notes when encountered, but do not include them in the candidate ledger.
