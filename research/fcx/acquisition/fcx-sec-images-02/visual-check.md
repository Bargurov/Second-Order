a text layer, which the package's quotes were matched against (`research/fcx/sec-match.md`). The three images (this
approval, n=1 to 3, retained beside the exhibit) were compared with that text on 2026-11-11, by reading each page.

- **Page 1.** Every passage the package cites on page 1 appears in the image as quoted: the dateline, the board
  approval, the doubling of mining and milling rates, the second-largest mine, the mine's history, the autonomous fleet,
  the early works, the discretionary classification and the funding schedule. The figures 20% (2027) and 30-35% (2028)
  also appear.
- **Page 2.**
  - The passages on the cost estimates, the aim and the schedule appear in the image.
  - The figures appear as recorded: over 400 and approximately 225 million pounds of copper; over 20 and approximately
    12 million pounds of molybdenum; $4.5 billion; approximately 115,000 tons per day; approximately 3.5 years with
    initial production in mid-2030; an approximate 20% reduction in unit net cash costs; approximately $4.00 per pound
    of copper ($20 per pound of molybdenum); and 2.6 billion metric tons of ore at 0.43% copper equivalent grade.
- **Page 3.** The sentence on the operational estimates being subject to the quarterly closing process appears in the
  image.

No difference was found between the visible pages and the text layer for any item the package records.
