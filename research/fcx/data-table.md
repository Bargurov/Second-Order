## Assumptions

- `a-price-volume-split`: The change in copper sales value is split with the volume effect valued at the earlier period's realized price and the price effect at the later period's volume; another order would split it differently, and the two parts always add up to the total change. Used by: `c-cu-price-effect-q3-2026`, `c-cu-volume-effect-q3-2026`, `c-cu-price-effect-9m-2026`, `c-cu-volume-effect-9m-2026`, `c-cu-sales-value-q3-2026`, `c-cu-sales-value-q3-2025`, `c-cu-sales-value-9m-2026`, `c-cu-sales-value-9m-2025`, `c-cu-price-effect-fy2025`, `c-cu-volume-effect-fy2025`, `c-cu-sales-value-fy2025`, `c-cu-sales-value-fy2024`.
- `a-outlook-face-value`: The company's "approximately" outlook figures are taken as printed; their rounding carries into the implied fourth-quarter figures, and the outlook rests on the company's price assumptions for Q4 2026 ($6.50/lb copper, $4,000/oz gold, $32.00/lb molybdenum). Used by: `c-q4-ocf-implied`, `c-q4-capex-implied`.
- `a-q4-price-arithmetic`: Every pound of the Q4 2026 copper sales outlook is priced $0.10 higher, with no change in volumes, costs, taxes, royalties or timing. Used by: `c-q4-copper-revenue-per-10c`.
- `a-scenario-sales-value`: Copper sales value is approximated as price times volume and nothing else: every pound sold at one price. Reported revenue is a different measure, and so are earnings and cash flow; the Scenarios tab's change in this value is split as in a-price-volume-split. Used by: `c-q4-cu-sales-value-base`.
- `a-q4-2025-same-basis`: The release's 9M 2025 comparatives are on the same basis as the 10-K's FY2025 figures, so their difference is the fourth quarter of 2025. For cash flows this is not assumed, because the 10-Q presents some 2025 cash flow figures that differ from the release's (c-ocf-difference-9m-2025). Used by: `c-cu-sales-q4-2025`, `c-revenues-q4-2025`.
- `a-revenue-bridge-grouping`: Groups the lines of the company's revenue bridge under the company's own two headings, sales volumes and realized prices (copper, gold and molybdenum), and puts every other line in a third group, "other lines". The company prints no group totals; the grouping of the other lines is this project's. Used by: `c-rev-bridge-volume-q3-2026`, `c-rev-bridge-price-q3-2026`, `c-rev-bridge-other-lines-q3-2026`, `c-rev-bridge-volume-9m-2026`, `c-rev-bridge-price-9m-2026`, `c-rev-bridge-other-lines-9m-2026`, `c-rev-bridge-volume-fy2025`, `c-rev-bridge-price-fy2025`, `c-rev-bridge-other-lines-fy2025`.
- `a-bagdad-funding-arithmetic`: Applies the company's stated funding shares to its $4.5 billion estimate. The company gives shares, not amounts; the amounts change if the estimate changes, and 'the balance in 2029-2030' is not split between the two years. Used by: `c-bagdad-funding-2027`, `c-bagdad-funding-2028-low`, `c-bagdad-funding-2028-high`, `c-bagdad-funding-2029-2030-low`, `c-bagdad-funding-2029-2030-high`.
- `a-bagdad-scale-reference`: Sets the company's expected added copper (a long-term annual rate after the project reaches full rates, from mid-2030 at the earliest) against FY2025 copper sales, only to show its size. It is not a forecast of FCX's future volumes or of Bagdad's share of them: other mines will change too. Used by: `c-bagdad-cu-increment-share-fy2025`, `c-bagdad-cu-increment-share-net-fy2025`.
