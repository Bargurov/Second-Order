## In one paragraph

Freeport-McMoRan describes itself as "a leading international metals company with the objective of being foremost in
copper" (`k25-overview-objective`). It mines copper in three regions:

- the United States (Arizona and New Mexico);
- South America (Peru and Chile);
- Indonesia (the Grasberg minerals district).

Gold comes almost only from Grasberg, and molybdenum mostly from the U.S.

In 2025 copper was 75% of consolidated revenues, gold 15% and molybdenum 8% (`k25-revenue-share-copper-fy2025`,
`k25-revenue-share-gold-fy2025`, `k25-revenue-share-molybdenum-fy2025`).

The company consolidates partly owned mines, so its reported figures include partners' shares. FCX owns 48.76% of
Grasberg's operating company (PTFI), 55.66% of Cerro Verde since May 2026, 51% of El Abra, and 72% of Morenci.

## 2. Regions, divisions and segments

- **Divisions:** four, "U.S. copper mines, South America operations, Indonesia operations and Molybdenum mines"
  (`k25-divisions`).
- **Reportable segments:** Cerro Verde, Indonesia operations and U.S. Rod & Refining (`k25-reportable-segments`), plus
  Morenci and Atlantic Copper, disclosed voluntarily (`k25-voluntary-segments`).
- **Concentration:** in 2025 the three material mines produced 70% of consolidated copper (Morenci, Cerro Verde and the
  Grasberg minerals district; `k25-three-mines-share-copper-production-fy2025`, `k25-material-mines`).
- **Reserves at 2025-12-31 (copper):** U.S. 38%, South America 40%, Indonesia 22% (`k25-reserve-alloc-copper-us-2025-12-31`,
  `k25-reserve-alloc-copper-south-america-2025-12-31`, `k25-reserve-alloc-copper-indonesia-2025-12-31`).
  - *Reading:* Indonesia's reserve share is below its production share. One stated factor fits: its reserves are
    counted only through 2041, as the IUPK runs (`k25-reserves-indonesia-through-2041`). The 10-K does not say how
    much of the difference this explains.

## 4. How exposed FCX is to copper, and on what basis

- **Revenue:** copper is three quarters of revenue (`k25-revenue-share-copper-fy2025`). In 2025 the copper sales value
  was about $17.0 billion (`c-cu-sales-value-fy2025`, computed as sales volume × realized price).
- **Pricing:** U.S. copper is priced off COMEX, with a premium for cathode and rod (`k25-us-cathode-rod-pricing`).
  South America and Indonesia are priced off the LME (`k25-lme-basis-sa-indonesia`). 2025 averages: LME $4.51, COMEX
  $4.82 a pound (`k25-lme-copper-average-fy2025`, `k25-comex-copper-average-fy2025`).
- **Consolidated against FCX's share:**
  - 2025 copper sales were 3,574 million lb consolidated (`k25-copper-sales-fy2025`) and 2,467 million lb net of
    noncontrolling interests (`k25-copper-sales-net-fy2025`).
  - So 69.0% of the consolidated pounds are FCX's (`c-net-share-cu-sales-fy2025`). In Q3 2026 it was 73.7%
    (`c-net-share-cu-sales-q3-2026`).
  - The attribution rule: "Beginning January 1, 2023, the attribution of PTFI's net income or loss is based on equity
    ownership percentages" (`k25-ptfi-attribution`). Before 2023 FCX's economic interest was about 81%
    (`k25-ptfi-economic-interest`).
- **By-products lower the reported unit cost:** gold and molybdenum revenue is netted against copper costs
  (`k25-by-product-method`). Indonesia's 2025 unit figure was a net credit of $0.55 a pound
  (`k25-ptfi-unit-net-cash-credits-fy2025`). The U.S. mines' unit net cash costs were $3.05 a pound
  (`k25-us-unit-net-cash-costs-fy2025`), and South America's $2.43 (`k25-sa-unit-net-cash-costs-fy2025`).
  - The release calls PTFI's underground operations "among the lowest-cost copper mining operations in the world"
    (`r3-p-low-cost`).
  - *Reading:* on the company's unit-cost measure, Grasberg supplies both the lowest-cost copper and nearly all the
    gold. When Grasberg is down, FCX loses its cheapest pounds and its by-product credit together. The company's own
    reason for higher 2026 unit costs starts with "lower copper and gold volumes at PTFI" (`r3-p-unc-reason`). It
    gives no split of how much each part contributed.

## 5. The September 2025 mud rush

- **What happened:**
  - "On September 8, 2025, PTFI experienced an external mud rush incident that resulted in seven fatalities"
    (`k25-mud-rush-event`).
  - About 800,000 tonnes of wet material entered the Grasberg Block Cave mine from the former open pit
    (`k25-mud-rush-mechanism`).
- **The restart sequence:**
  - **Late October 2025:** the DMLZ and Big Gossan mines restarted (`k25-mud-rush-dmlz-restart`).
  - **Q4 2025:** smelting in Indonesia was suspended for lack of concentrate (`k25-mud-rush-smelters-suspended`).
  - **End of March 2026:** Production Blocks 2 and 3 began their ramp-up (`q3-p-gbc-blocks-2-3`).
  - **April 2026:** PTFI collected pre-tax insurance proceeds of $0.7 billion (`q3-p-insurance-proceeds`).
  - **Late August 2026:** PTFI's smelter restarted (`r3-p-smelter`).
- **Status in Q3 2026:**
  - Mills ran at 67% of capacity (`r3-mill-capacity-share-q3-2026`).
  - The company expects 80% in mid-2027 and near full capacity by year-end 2027 (`r3-p-ramp-up`).
- **The earlier expectation:** the 10-K expected about 85% of normal production restored in the second half of 2026
  (`k25-mud-rush-85pct`).
  - *Reading:* by October 2026, the timetable to full capacity reads later than the 10-K's. The two statements use
    different measures (production against mill capacity), so this is not a like-for-like slip.
- **Force majeure:** PTFI notified certain counterparties of force majeure (`k25-mud-rush-force-majeure`).

## 6. The Indonesian framework

- **Mining rights:** the IUPK grants mining rights through 2031, with rights to extend through 2041
  (`k25-iupk-term`).
- **Fiscal terms:** "a 25% corporate income tax rate, a 10% profits tax on net income, and royalty rates of 4% for
  copper, 3.75% for gold and 3.25% for silver" (`k25-iupk-fiscal-terms`).
- **Exports:**
  - The concentrate export licence expired on 2025-09-16 (`k25-export-licence-expired`), so concentrate is now
    smelted in Indonesia.
  - Refined gold exports face a 10% duty under current regulations, which conflicts with the IUPK
    (`r3-p-gold-export`).
- **Cash rules:** 100% of export proceeds must be deposited in Indonesian banks for 12 months
  (`k25-export-proceeds-rule`). They may be used for business needs, including dividends and taxes
  (`q3-p-export-proceeds-100pct`).
- **Beyond 2041:** under a 2026 MOU, FCX would keep 48.76% through 2041 and hold about 37% from 2042 (`r3-p-mou`).
  PTFI applied for the extension in June 2026 (`q3-p-iupk-extension`).
- **Tax disputes:** in October 2026 PTFI received about $740 million of tax assessments for 2023
  (`q3-p-tax-assessments-2023`).

## 7. The Bagdad expansion (announced 2026-11-04)

- **Decided:** the board "approved a $4.5 billion expansion and modernization program" for Bagdad, FCX's wholly owned
  copper and molybdenum mine in Arizona (`nr-p-board-approval`, `q3-p-bagdad-approval`).
- **Company estimates, not results:**
  - over 400 million pounds of copper a year in the long term, about 225 million of it incremental
    (`nr-bagdad-cu-total`, `nr-bagdad-cu-increment`);
  - initial production targeted in mid-2030, after about 3.5 years of construction (`nr-p-schedule`);
  - Bagdad would become the second-largest U.S. copper mine, behind Morenci (`nr-p-second-largest`).
- **Why it matters for exposure:** Bagdad is wholly owned, so its added pounds would count in full in FCX's net
  share. They would be U.S. pounds, which the company prices off COMEX (`k25-us-cathode-rod-pricing`). *Reading:* both
  follow from the stated ownership and pricing rules. Neither is a figure the company gives, and other mines' volumes
  will also change by 2030.
- Cost, funding and the earlier estimates are in `mechanisms.md` (section 7) and `comparison.md`.
