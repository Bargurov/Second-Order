assistant extracted the release. These are the subagents' observations as they wrote them: definitions, caveats
and inconsistencies met while recording figures. Page numbers are as each note states them. The figures they
mention are in `facts.json`, and `gaps.md` takes up the ones that matter.

## Form 10-K 2025 (`10k-2025`)

- Unit net cash costs are non-GAAP (company definition): by-product method, net of gold/molybdenum/silver credits, before DD&A and 'noncash and other costs' (impairments, idle facility costs, ARO accretion, feasibility study and start-up costs, etc.). Consolidated 2025 $1.65/lb (2024 $1.56) and site costs $2.75/lb exclude $0.17/lb ($625 million) of mud rush idle facility costs and direct recovery expenses. Pages 93, 95, 115 (printed 91, 93, 113).
- 'Consolidated' volumes and reserves include 100% of PTFI (48.76%-owned), Cerro Verde (55.08%) and El Abra (51%), but only FCX's 72% of Morenci (proportionate consolidation; partners take in kind, so not NCI). Net-of-NCI rows: 2025 copper production 3,383 consolidated vs 2,376 net; sales 3,574 vs 2,467. Consolidated sales exclude purchased copper (127 million lb in 2025). Pages 36, 100.
- PTFI: FCX's share ownership and economic interest are both 48.76% from 2023 (economic interest approx. 81% through 2022). FCX consolidates PTFI despite minority ownership because it controls the operating committee (Reg S-X Rule 3A-02). FCX expects ~49% through 2041 and ~37% from 2042. 2024 tax-settlement income was attributed ~81% to FCX. Pages 24, 99, 139-140.
- Only one price-sensitivity table (MD&A Outlook, page 86): all three figures change consolidated OPERATING CASH FLOWS for the year 2026 — $330m per $0.10/lb copper, $75m per $100/oz gold, $160m per $2/lb molybdenum (per $2, not $1). Base: $5.00/lb copper, $4,000/oz gold, $20.00/lb moly; OCF ~$8bn incl. $1bn working capital and other sources and a $2.7bn tax provision. No EBITDA or net-income sensitivity except NCI net income: $0.2bn per $0.25/lb copper (page 96). Unit-cost sensitivities are separate ($0.03/lb per $100/oz gold; $0.03/lb per $2/lb moly).
- Two different open provisional copper figures at 2025-12-31: 152 million lb at $5.64/lb, net of intercompany sales and NCI (MD&A market risk, page 113), vs a 365 million lb gross embedded-derivative position (contract $5.05, market $5.64, maturing through May 2026) in Note 12 (page 168). Do not mix them.
- U.S. copper mines sales differ between tables: 1,296 (2025) / 1,257 (2024) million lb in operating data (page 101) vs 1,301 / 1,263 in the unit cost tables (pages 102, 116). The 10-K does not explain the gap.
- The ownership-interest table on printed page 2 (page 4) is missing from the page text (only its footnotes survive). Ownership figures were taken from the mine descriptions, the production table (page 36) and Note 2 (page 139) instead.
- The 10-K never uses the name 'Manyar': PTFI's new smelter and PMR are described as in Gresik / Eastern Java and wholly owned by PTFI; PT Smelting is 66% PTFI-owned (equity method). PTFI smelter capacity is about 1.7 million t of concentrate a year, and shipments to it are expected to resume in 2H 2026. Pages 25, 28, 109, 139.
- Not printed: a separate 'FCX stockholders’ equity' label (the balance sheet shows 'Total stockholders’ equity' 18,899, NCI 11,867, total equity 30,766; page 132); any percentage of consolidated copper 'net of NCI' (only absolute net volumes, page 36); and a historical major vs sustaining capex split. For 2025, only major mining projects ($2.3bn) and PTFI downstream ($0.6bn) are named (page 112); the $3.0bn/$1.3bn split exists only for the 2026 outlook (page 86).
- Net debt (non-GAAP) = consolidated debt less consolidated cash: $5,555m at 2025-12-31, or $2,320m excluding $3,235m of PTFI downstream debt. The financial-policy target is $3-4bn, excluding PTFI downstream project debt. Page 114.
- Reserves use $3.25/lb copper, $1,600/oz gold and $14.00/lb moly. Consolidated reserves are after the Morenci JV reduction; net equity is further reduced for NCI (112.3 vs 78.6 billion lb copper). Indonesia reserves run only to 2041 (IUPK), and 34% of them are expected to be mined before end-2031. Pages 38-39, 60.
- Export duties for 2023 are printed as $324m in Note 11 (page 166) but (307) in the Note 14 revenue adjustments (page 172); FY2025/FY2024 agree (337/457). The income-statement NCI line and other deductions are printed in parentheses and recorded as negative values.
- MD&A Summary Financial Data (page 92) repeats the audited amounts (revenues, operating income, net income attributable, diluted EPS, operating cash flows); only its 'Capital expenditures' 4,494 and 'Total debt, including current portion' 9,379 are not single lines in the statements. South America 2026 sales are '1.1 billion' in the text (page 103) vs 1,080 in the outlook table (page 85), a rounding difference.

## Form 10-Q Q3 2026 (`10q-2026q3`)

- Unit net cash costs are non-GAAP. They use the by-product method (net of gold, molybdenum and silver credits), before DD&A and net noncash and other costs, and exclude mud rush idle facility, direct recovery and restoration costs: $0.33/lb in Q3 2026, $0.44/lb in 9M 2026 at the consolidated level (pp.29, 31, 52). Indonesia appears as net credits, e.g. (0.84) in Q3 2026 (p.45); PTFI’s 2026 outlook figure of $1.25 is a credit (p.47).
- The income statement has no separate idle-facility/mud-rush cost line (p.4). The costs sit inside production and delivery ($244m Q3 2026; $934m 9M 2026) and DD&A ($64m; $236m) (pp.19, 21, 31, 36). The only mud-rush line is the $(699)m insurance gain in 9M 2026. Wording differs: Note 8 says 'idle facility and restoration costs' for 2026 (pp.19, 21), MD&A says 'idle facility, direct recovery and restoration costs' (p.31).
- The 10-Q's only price sensitivity for cash flow is to consolidated operating cash flows for FY2026, from price changes during Q4 2026 only: $75m per $0.10/lb copper, $25m per $100/oz gold, $12m per $2/lb molybdenum, on a base of $6.50/lb, $4,000/oz and $32.00/lb (p.25; Item 3 refers back to it, p.68). It is not annual and there is no EBITDA sensitivity. Unit-cost sensitivities are separate (pp.24, 40, 42, 47).
- Two different figures for provisionally priced copper: MD&A gives 239 million lb (134 million lb net of NCI) open at $6.55/lb (p.30); Note 5 gives 256 million lb of copper embedded derivatives in provisional sales contracts, contract $6.34 and market $6.55 (p.13). The 10-Q does not reconcile them.
- Labels as printed: the statements are titled 'Consolidated ... (Unaudited)', not 'Condensed'. The equity line reads 'Total stockholders’ equity', not 'Total FCX stockholders’ equity' (p.3). The income statement says 'Net income attributable to common stockholders' (p.4), MD&A says 'attributable to common stock' (p.28). Note 2's EPS numerator ($1,166m for Q3 2026) differs from the income-statement $1,174m because it deducts amounts allocated to participating securities (p.9).
- The cash-flow statement prints no working-capital subtotal (p.6). MD&A gives cash used for working capital: $0.4bn Q3 2026, $0.8bn 9M 2026 (p.28), and calls the 9M figure 'working capital and other uses' (p.50). 9M 2026 operating cash flow of $5,524m includes the $699m insurance proceeds (pp.6, 50). Quarterly operating cash flow ($1,981m Q3 2026) appears only in MD&A (p.28).
- PTFI: FCX states a 48.76% ownership interest and consolidates PTFI (p.42). Under the MOU this becomes about 37% from 2042 (p.43). The 10-Q gives no 'economic interest' figure and does not explain how NCI shares in PTFI profits. What it does give is NCI by segment: Indonesia $239m in Q3 2026 and $706m in 9M 2026 (pp.34-35), and Cerro Verde $266m and $662m. All 9M 2026 NCI distributions related to Cerro Verde; there were none from PTFI (p.50).
- Downstream debt carve-out: $3,237m = PTFI senior notes ($2,987m) plus the PTFI revolver ($250m) (pp.10, 52). MD&A rounds it to $3.2bn (p.23). Net debt excluding it is $1,826m against a $3.0-4.0bn target (non-GAAP; pp.49, 52). There was no Cerro Verde debt at 2026-09-30 (p.11). 9M 2026 interest included $67m of nonrecurring withholding-tax adjustments on PTFI notes, of which $64m was capitalized (p.11).
- Consolidated volumes include 100% of PTFI, Cerro Verde (55.66%) and El Abra (51%), and FCX’s 72% share of Morenci (pp.37, 40, 42). Only the open provisional sales are also given net of NCI (p.30). The U.S. operating data table is labelled 'Net of Joint Venture Interests' (p.38).
- Production-vs-sales gap: in Q3 2026 PTFI produced 249m lb of copper but sold 153m lb, and produced 229 koz of gold but sold 98 koz (p.44). Refined sales are now recognized only after smelting and refining. About 60 koz of gold slipped from Q3 into Q4 (p.45), and full-year production is expected to exceed sales by ~100m lb Cu and ~70 koz Au (p.24). The balance sheet shows consolidated product inventories of $3,769m vs $3,332m but does not split out PTFI (p.3).
- Grasberg ramp-up: Q3 2026 mill throughput was 141 kt/d (about 67% of capacity), including 70 kt/d from the Grasberg Block Cave (narrative, p.43). The table shows 141,300 and 69,500 t/d (p.44), so the narrative is rounded. Targets: 80% of capacity by mid-2027, near full capacity by end-2027, PB1S restart by mid-2027 (p.43).
- Most Morenci segment revenue is intersegment ($864m of $883m in Q3 2026, p.19). The total-segment-revenue row has no label in the page text: it is the six values after the Intersegment row.
- Taxes: the 9M 2026 effective tax rate was 29% vs 36%, helped by a PTFI smelter tax incentive (2025-2030) confirmed in Q3 2026 that included 2025 benefits (pp.10, 32). A further ~$740m of PTFI tax assessments for 2023 was received in October 2026 and PTFI plans to object (p.17).
